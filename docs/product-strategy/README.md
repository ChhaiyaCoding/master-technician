**Core thesis:** MT is not an AI chatbot and not an always-open app — it's an
**instant professional-reference tool** that delivers the right technical
information at the right moment, then gets out of the way. It **reduces searching
time, not screen time**: *open → find in seconds → close → keep repairing.* Each
saved case makes the next diagnosis faster (the flywheel). It stays **mobile-first,
Khmer-first, and diagnosis-and-knowledge only** (never garage management / POS,
never engagement mechanics).
