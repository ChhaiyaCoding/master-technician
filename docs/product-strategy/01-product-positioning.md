# 01 — Product Positioning

## What is Master Technician?

**Master Technician is an instant professional-assistance tool for automotive
technicians** — a mobile-first, Khmer-first source of trusted technical information
that a mechanic reaches for at the exact moment a decision is needed, and then puts
down to keep working on the vehicle.

It is **not** a chatbot with a car theme. It is a **structured diagnostic and
knowledge system** with AI inside it. The AI is one component; the product is the
speed, the trustworthiness, and the memory of every repair the technician has done.

> One-line positioning: *"Get the right technical answer in seconds — then get back
> to the car. Find the fault, fix it right, stay safe, and never re-solve the same
> problem twice."*

## The core philosophy: reduce searching time, not screen time

Professional mechanics spend almost all of their time **on the vehicle** — tools,
measurements, scan tools, repairs. The phone is **not** the workspace; it is a
**reference instrument** picked up only when information is needed. So the product
is measured by how *little* time the mechanic spends in it:

> **Open → find in seconds → close → keep repairing.**
> The goal is **minimal screen time, maximum repair time.**

Master Technician wins by **removing friction between a question and a trusted
answer**, not by holding attention.

## Why does it exist?

Professional diagnosis today is slow and lossy:

- Knowledge lives in **people's heads, group chats, and scattered notes** — and
  walks out the door when a senior tech leaves.
- Reference data (DTCs, procedures, specs, wiring) is **expensive, desktop-bound,
  English-only, and slow to search** — a laptop trip that interrupts the repair.
- Generic AI tools **guess confidently and can be dangerously wrong** about
  vehicles, with no safety guardrails for high-voltage or airbag work.
- Every workshop **re-solves the same faults repeatedly** because there is no
  fast, searchable memory of real repairs.

Master Technician exists to **collapse the time from question to trusted answer**,
to **preserve hard-won repair knowledge**, and to do it **in the technician's
language, on the device already in their hand** — in seconds, not minutes.

## What problems does it solve?

1. **Slow searching for information** → the required answer in seconds, then close.
2. **Uncertain diagnosis** → guided workflow + ranked causes with evidence.
3. **Wrong-part replacement ("parts cannon")** → confirm-before-replace steps.
4. **Knowledge loss** → each repair becomes a searchable case; shop experience
   compounds instead of evaporating.
5. **Reference friction** → DTC meanings, inspection flows, common mistakes (and
   later procedures/specs/wiring) on a phone, in Khmer, fast.
6. **Safety risk** → automatic HV (Hybrid/EV) and SRS/Airbag warnings at the moment
   of the work.
7. **Language barrier** → Khmer-first, English technical terms preserved.
8. **Repeated mistakes** → "common mistakes" and "similar cases" prevent known traps.

## Why is it different from…

### …ChatGPT / generic AI chatbots
- **General AI guesses; Master Technician refuses to guess.** It asks structured
  follow-up questions when data is missing, ranks causes *with evidence*, and cites
  the DTC/symptom/reading behind each conclusion (see `../ai-system.md`).
- **Information over conversation.** A chatbot maximizes dialogue; MT minimizes it —
  it surfaces a scannable answer so the tech can leave quickly.
- **Safety is enforced**, not optional — HV/SRS warnings are mandatory outputs.
- **Structured workflow + memory.** A chatbot forgets; MT turns each job into a
  reusable case and grounds answers in the shop's own history and DTC base.
- **Built for seconds at the bench:** one-hand mobile UX, Khmer-first, offline-
  capable direction — not a blank text box that invites a long chat.

### …ALLDATA
- ALLDATA is a **deep OEM reference library** — powerful but **desktop-first,
  subscription-heavy, English-only**, and slow to navigate mid-repair.
- Master Technician is **fast, mobile, and workflow-first**: it delivers the
  relevant answer in seconds and **learns from the tech's repairs**. Curated
  reference (procedures/wiring/specs) is a *layer* inside our fast workflow (see
  `05-knowledge-roadmap.md`), not a desktop library trip.

### …Autodata
- Autodata excels at **technical/repair-time data** for professionals — again
  **reference-centric and largely desktop/region-licensed**.
- We differ by being the **fast on-demand assistant** in the diagnostic loop
  (ranked causes, confirm-before-replace, safety) plus a **living repair-case
  memory**, delivered in the local language on mobile, optimized for time-to-answer.

### …Haynes (and other manuals)
- Haynes is a **static manual** — model-specific and **not adaptive**. It tells you
  *how* to do a job but not *what's wrong with this car right now*, and finding the
  page is slow.
- Master Technician is **dynamic and diagnostic**: it reasons from *this vehicle's*
  symptoms and codes, ranks likely causes, and gets you the relevant slice fast.

### …Scan tool apps (OBD dongles / scanner apps)
- Scan tools **read codes and live data** — essential, but **a code is not a
  diagnosis**. They stop at "P0301 present."
- Master Technician **starts where the scan tool ends**: interprets the code in
  context, ranks causes, gives an ordered inspection flow, warns of common mistakes,
  and captures the outcome. (Ingesting scan-tool data is an input to us, not a rival.)

## Why would a mechanic reach for this app?

Not because it wants their attention — because it **gives back time on every job**.
A mechanic opens Master Technician when they hit a question they can't answer from
memory, and the app rewards them by answering it in seconds:

- **"What does this code actually mean here?"** → DTC lookup, then back to the car.
- **"What's the most likely cause and what do I check next?"** → ranked causes +
  the next test.
- **"Is this safe to touch?"** → HV/airbag warning appears before the work.
- **"Have we seen this before?"** → similar case surfaces in seconds.
- **"How do I not forget this fix?"** → save the case in a few taps.

The relationship is one of **trust and speed**, not habit or screen time. The
mechanic values Master Technician precisely because it lets them **spend less time
on the phone and more time fixing the vehicle** — faster fixes, fewer comebacks,
safer work, and a knowledge base that makes them better over time.
